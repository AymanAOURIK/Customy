# Tailoring Style Guide

Use this as the internal quality bar for resume content. It is a reference for tone, density, and structure, not a source to copy from.

## Target Outcome

- one page
- dense and ATS-friendly
- credible senior positioning
- direct wording
- no application fluff

## Title Pattern

- Prefer a clean hybrid role title that matches the JD without sounding copied badly.
- Good: `Lead Tech Data & IA`
- Bad: `En tant que Lead`
- Bad: `As a Tech Lead`

## Summary Pattern

- Two compact sentences.
- Sentence one: establish level and domain.
- Sentence two: show scope, production ownership, leadership, and business impact.
- Imply alignment. Do not say `strong fit`, `ideal candidate`, or similar phrases.

## Experience Pattern

- Put the strongest proof for the JD first.
- Preserve facts, tools, metrics, and outcomes.
- Rephrase for clarity and impact when useful.
- Prefer bullets that sound delivered, shipped, led, designed, deployed, industrialized.

## Skills Pattern

- Reorder skills to mirror the JD.
- Use only true skills from `candidate.yaml`.
- Favor relevant hard skills first.
- Keep soft skills concise and secondary.

## Language Pattern

- French JD -> natural professional French.
- English JD -> direct professional English.
- Avoid literal translation artifacts.

## Banned Phrases

- `strong fit`
- `making me a strong fit`
- `En tant que`
- `As a`
- `I am excited to apply`
- `ideal candidate`
- `perfect fit`
